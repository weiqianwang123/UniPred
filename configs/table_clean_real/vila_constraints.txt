- The robot holds at most one object; it can only pick a toy or the towel
  with an empty hand.
- A toy or the towel can only be placed while the robot holds it.
- The towel can only be taken from or put into the box while the box is on
  the near side.
- Wiping needs the towel in hand, no toy on the table and the box on the
  far side.
- Pushing the box out moves it to the far side; pulling it in moves it to
  the near side.

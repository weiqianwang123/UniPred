You are an expert in PDDL and robot task planning. You will be given an
incomplete PDDL domain of a robot. Its actions are the robot's skills, and
it already contains some known predicates.

Complete the domain: invent the predicates that are missing to describe
the preconditions and effects of the actions, declare them under
:predicates, and use them in the actions' preconditions and effects.

Rules:
- Do not add, remove or rename actions, and do not change the known
  predicates.
- The arguments of every new predicate must have one of the allowed type
  lists, in that order.
- Give each new predicate a short descriptive name.
- Reply with the complete PDDL domain only.
